# What goes in this repository

**In short:** lect-kit is public and holds none of your language. Your language stays
with your community, in places you control.

## Why

Anything put in a public repository can be copied, stored and used to train AI models,
and deleting it later doesn't undo that. Languages, names and restricted knowledge
belong to their communities, so none of it goes here.

## What's here

- The kit's code and the shape of its data.
- Explanations of how the kit works, like these pages.
- A worked example in Japanese, from texts in the public domain.
- Abstract test data that doesn't resemble any language.

## What's never here

- Words, sentences or translations from the language of any community using the kit.
- The names of speakers, families or informants.
- Restricted or sensitive knowledge, or lists of restricted words.
- Recordings, transcripts, page images or archive references.
- The decisions and history of any community's project.
- Personal details such as email addresses or computer paths.

## How it's checked

Every change is read in full before it's added. A checking tool also looks for anything
that shouldn't be here. When your community's people work on the kit, the tool can check
against your own lists of words and restricted terms, which it reads from your own
storage. It never copies them, stores them here or shows what it found, only where.

The tool only finds what it's told to look for, so the reading matters more. If you see
something here that shouldn't be, please tell the project custodian.
